# The order-four permutahedron

The source constructs the convex hull of the 24 coordinate permutations of
`(1,2,3,4)`. Although the points live in four coordinates, their coordinate
sum is fixed, so the hull has affine dimension three.

Noether constructs and verifies the exact rational polytope. Its face lattice
has f-vector `[1,24,36,14,1]`: the empty face, 24 vertices, 36 edges,
14 facets, and the full polytope. This extends the corpus's order-three example
without reproducing the source's plotting layer.
