# A generalized mixed discriminant

For three generic `3 x 3` matrices `A`, `B`, and `C`, the paper identifies
their generalized mixed discriminant with the coefficient of `xyz` in
`det(I+xA+yB+zC)`. Noether computes the determinant coefficient and compares
it with the independent 36-term signed sum over column permutations and
assignments of `A`, `B`, and `C` to the three rows.
