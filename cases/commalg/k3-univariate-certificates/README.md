# Exact univariate certificates from a degree-two K3 computation

Three bounded exact calculations occur inside the source's larger K3-surface
workflow:

1. factor a degree-22 Tate-normalized Weil polynomial and identify its sole
   cyclotomic factor `(x-1)^2`;
2. prove that two restricted sextics over `F_5` and `F_31` are squares and
   recover roots of degree at most three; and
3. isolate the only real root of a degree-25 critical-value eliminant and
   certify that it is strictly negative.

Noether verifies all three using exact polynomial arithmetic and returned
certificates. This does not claim the much larger point-count, Frobenius,
elimination, or bad-prime searches from which the univariate inputs arose.
