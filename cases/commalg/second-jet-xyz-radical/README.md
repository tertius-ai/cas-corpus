# The second jet of the hypersurface xyz=0

Substitute

```text
x(t)=x0+x1*t+x2*t^2,
y(t)=y0+y1*t+y2*t^2,
z(t)=z0+z1*t+z2*t^2
```

into `x*y*z`, retain the coefficients through `t^2`, and form their ideal
`J`. Compute and certify `radical(J)`. It must be the squarefree monomial ideal
generated by the ten terms occurring in those three coefficients. Finally,
certify that `J` has ten minimal primes, as in the paper.

This explicit expansion avoids claiming a general jet-scheme constructor and
does not reproduce the source's local multiplicity calculations.
