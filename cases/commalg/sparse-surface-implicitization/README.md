# Implicitize a sparse surface

For parameters `s,t`, consider the four polynomials

```text
f1 = s*t^6 + 2
f2 = s*t^5 - 3*s*t^3
f3 = s*t^4 + 5*s^2*t^6
f4 = 2 + s^2*t^6.
```

Introduce a projective scale `u`, form the graph ideal
`(T1-u*f1,...,T4-u*f4)`, and eliminate `s,t,u`. Certify that the elimination
ideal is principal and generated by a homogeneous polynomial of degree six
with 41 terms. Compare it coefficient-for-coefficient with the equation in the
paper.

Two terms in the arXiv source are printed with total degree seven:
`157155*T1*T2^2*T3^3*T4` and `-19470*T1^2*T3*T4^4`. Homogeneity and direct
elimination independently give exponents `T3^2` and `T4^3`; with those two
corrections, the result agrees exactly up to the paper's scalar factor `2809`.
This case computes the same implicit equation but not the source's
approximation-complex representation matrix.
