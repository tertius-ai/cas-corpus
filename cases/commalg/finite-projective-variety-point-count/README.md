# A projective intersection of quadrics over F5

In projective 5-space over `F_5`, consider the common zero locus of

`x1^2+x2^2+x3^2+x4^2+x5^2+x6^2`

and

`x1*x2+x3*x4+x5*x6`.

Noether normalizes projective coordinates, partitions the finite enumeration
into four deterministic shards, and finds exactly 156 rational points. The
returned certificate records all 3,906 normalized projective candidates,
per-shard counts and checksums, and verifies against the defining scheme.

This case translates the source's bounded algebraic-variety example, not its
broader finite-incidence-geometry functionality.
