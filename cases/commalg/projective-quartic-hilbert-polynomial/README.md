# Hilbert polynomial of a projective quartic curve

In projective three-space over `Q`, let `X` be cut out by

```text
xw - yz,
yw - (x-z)(x-2z).
```

Compute its projective dimension, degree, and Hilbert polynomial. The source
reports a smooth genus-one quartic with Hilbert polynomial `4t`. Noether 0.2
recovers the exact polynomial `4m` and degree four. This scoped case does not
claim the source's smoothness calculation, because that certificate is not
available for this presentation in the pinned release.
