# Weighted critical ideal of the Dingdong surface

For `f=x^2+y^2+z^3-z^2`, data `(1,12,2)`, and weights `(1,1,2)`, the
paper displays four generators of the weighted critical ideal. Noether forms
the augmented-Jacobian minors, saturates away the singular locus, proves mutual
ideal containment with the displayed answer, and recovers degree five.
