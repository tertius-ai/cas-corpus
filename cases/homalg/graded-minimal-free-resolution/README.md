# A minimal resolution with Betti totals (1,5,5,1)

Let `R=Q[w,x,y,z]` and let `A=R/I`, where `I` is generated by

```text
w^2-xz, wx-yz, x^2-wy, xy-z^2, y^2-wz.
```

Regard `A` as the graded presented `R`-module with one degree-zero generator
and five quadratic relations. Compute a minimal graded free resolution and its
Betti table.

Noether certifies a resolution of length three with nonzero graded entries

```text
beta_(0,0)=1, beta_(1,2)=5, beta_(2,3)=5, beta_(3,5)=1.
```

Thus its total Betti ranks are `(1,5,5,1)`. This translates the corresponding
OSCAR example using Noether's native graded-module and resolution objects;
constructing an arbitrary chain complex with the expected ranks would not
establish generation, exactness, or minimality.
