# A strongly stable ideal from weighted Borel generators

Enter the nine minimal generators of the strongly stable ideal generated in
the source's weighted-Borel example, then resolve its quotient over
`Q[x1,x2,x3]`.

Noether verifies the pure graded Betti table
`1, 9 t^4, 13 t^5, 5 t^6`. This case begins after the Borel closure has been
formed and therefore makes no claim about weighted-Borel generation itself.
