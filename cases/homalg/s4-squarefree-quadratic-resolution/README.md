# The S4-stable squarefree quadratic ideal

Let `R=Q[x1,x2,x3,x4]` and let `I` be generated by all six squarefree
quadratic monomials. Compute and certify the minimal graded free resolution of
`R/I`. Its nonzero graded Betti entries must be

```text
beta_(0,0)=1, beta_(1,2)=6, beta_(2,3)=8, beta_(3,4)=3.
```

The ideal is preserved by permuting the four variables. This case recovers
the paper's ordinary Betti table, while its equivariant character refinement
remains unsupported.
