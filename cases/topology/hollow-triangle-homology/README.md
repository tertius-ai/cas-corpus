# Integral homology of a hollow triangle

Three vertices and the three edges joining them form a one-dimensional
simplicial complex with no filled triangle. It has simplex counts `(3,3)` and
Euler characteristic `3-3=0`.

Noether constructs the complex from its maximal edges and verifies

- `H_0 = Z`;
- `H_1 = Z`; and
- `H_2 = 0`.

Thus the hollow triangle has the integral homology of the circle, matching the
source's introductory `S^1` computation.
