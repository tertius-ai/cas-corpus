# Certify a fivefold symmetry of a rigidity graph

A FlexRiLoG notebook gives a 25-vertex, 60-edge framework and a permutation
written as five disjoint 5-cycles. Construct the exact graph and permutation,
verify that the permutation belongs to the full automorphism group, and
identify the cyclic subgroup it generates.

This is the combinatorial symmetry check underlying the notebook's geometric
motion; it does not claim to reproduce NAC colorings or the motion itself.
